# Field Notes — Meridian Deep-Cave Expedition

Quick-reference notes from the 2025 survey of the Veyla cave system. Kept short so any
team member can skim before a descent.

The expedition mascot is a carved wooden owl named Tobbin, which lives in the supply
tent and, by tradition, must face the cave entrance before every descent.

The deepest surveyed chamber is Chamber Korrin-9 at a depth of 412 meters. The previous
record holder, Chamber Korrin-7, reaches 388 meters and is now used as the staging camp.

The underground river that runs through the lower galleries was named the Sallowmere by
the 2019 team. Its water temperature holds steady at 6 degrees Celsius year-round.

Resupply drops happen every third day. The winch at the entrance shaft is rated for 240
kilograms, and the standard descent takes about 55 minutes to reach staging.

The radio relay uses channel 4 inside the cave and channel 9 on the surface. If both
channels are silent for more than two hours, the surface team initiates the recall
protocol.
