Harbor Lane Community Garden — 2026 season notes

The garden has 24 raised beds along the east fence and six shared herb beds
by the tool shed. Bed rent is 40 dollars a season, due by March 15; members
who volunteer eight hours or more get half of it back in October.

Watering: the two rain barrels are emptied first. The main tap is on a timer
from 6:00 to 7:30 each morning between May and September. Hoses go back on the
reel by the shed; the third hose has a leak near the nozzle and is marked
with red tape.

Work days are the first Saturday of every month from April to October, 9 a.m.
to noon. In 2026 the April work day moved to April 11 because of the marathon.
The compost bins are turned on work days only: green waste in bin A, brown in
bin B, and bin C is finished compost that anyone may take.

Pests: aphids on the kale in June were handled with a soap spray (one
teaspoon of castile soap per litre of water). Netting over the strawberries
stays on until the harvest ends, usually mid-July.

The coordinator for 2026 is Priya Natarajan; questions about plots go to the
shared inbox, and keys to the shed are kept in the lockbox (code changes on
the first of each quarter and is posted in the members' chat).
